Common questions

When should I expect delivery?

Made-to-order pieces typically take 4 to 6 weeks to deliver.

How is delivery priced?

The delivery fee for Johannesburg and surrounding areas is calculated at checkout. Deliveries beyond this area are quoted separately.

Can I explore different sizes or fabrics?

Selected pieces offer size and upholstery options in the Custom Studio. Modular designs can be arranged piece by piece.

Will materials look exactly as they do on screen?

Displays render colour differently, so previews are a guide. Visit the showroom to see and feel available materials.

What are my payment options?

Request a quote and arrange payment with our team, or pay online at checkout. Orders are released once payment has cleared.

Can you help with upholstery or room planning?

Explore the Services page for upholstery renewal and interior design support, from a focused update to a wider project.

Can I see materials in person?

You are welcome at our showroom during trading hours: Mon–Fri 09h00–16h00, Sat 09h00–14h00.

Delivery information

Made-to-order delivery typically takes 4 to 6 weeks from confirmation of payment. The applicable delivery fee is shown at checkout for Johannesburg and surrounding areas; other destinations are quoted separately. Contact the team for a delivery estimate.

Guarantee information

For current guarantee coverage and terms, contact our team before placing an order.

  • Normal wear, misuse, spills and accidental damage are not covered.
  • Claims require proof of purchase.
  • Repairs or replacements are at our discretion.

Ordering terms

Orders and payment

Orders are confirmed when payment or an agreed deposit has cleared. Items are released once the required funds have been received.

Made-to-order pieces

Custom and made-to-order pieces cannot be cancelled after production begins. Please contact the team to confirm any applicable cancellation terms before ordering.

Materials and dimensions

Material colours may vary slightly from screen previews. Any custom dimensions are confirmed in your quote before production.

Privacy

Information shared through this site is used to respond to enquiries, support accounts and process orders. Contact our team with questions about your information.
