Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Delivery on all items is between 4 and 6 weeks, as each piece is made to order.

What does delivery cost?

An automatic delivery fee is charged within Johannesburg and surrounding areas. The fee is shown at checkout.

Can I change the size or fabric?

Yes. On selected pieces the Custom Studio lets you adjust sizes and choose your fabric. Modular sofas can be built piece by piece.

Will the colours match what I see on screen?

Screens vary, so colours are approximations. Visit our showroom to see and feel real fabric swatches.

How do I pay?

You can request a quote and pay once it is agreed, or pay online at checkout. Items cannot be released until funds reflect in our account.

Do you offer re-upholstery or interior design?

Yes. See our Services page for re-upholstery and interior design, from a single room to a full home.

Can I visit the showroom?

You are welcome at our showroom during trading hours: Mon–Fri 09h00–16h00, Sat 09h00–14h00.
